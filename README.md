A complete, polished, playable browser arcade game built by my AI agent from ONE prompt. You are an exhausted AI robot in a chaotic digital office: the human has assigned too many tasks and everything is falling apart. Collect incoming tasks, dodge bugs, spam and exploding errors, grab coffee to restore energy, survive escalating waves and ever-changing difficulty modifiers — then face the final boss: a giant floating computer screen that says "HUMAN CHANGED THE REQUIREMENTS".

## How to play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser — no setup, no backend, no build step.

- **Move:** arrow keys or WASD on desktop, drag the virtual joystick on touch screens.
- **Collect:** touch task icons to earn points and build your combo multiplier.
- **Avoid:** bugs, spam emails, impossible requests and exploding error messages — they drain your AI stability and energy.
- **Coffee:** collect coffee power-ups to restore energy.
- **Survive:** waves get more chaotic; every 30 seconds a new difficulty modifier kicks in.
- **Boss:** defeat the final boss by collecting tasks while it is on screen.
- **Difficulty:** Easy, Normal or Hard on the title screen. Pause anytime; your best score is saved locally.
